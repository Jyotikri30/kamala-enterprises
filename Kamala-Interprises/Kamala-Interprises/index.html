    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions.

    What is the minimum order quantity?

    Minimum order quantities vary by product — our team will confirm exact quantities and lead times based on your project scope when you request a quote.

    Do you deliver outside your local region?

    Yes, we coordinate logistics for bulk orders across regions. Delivery timelines depend on order size and destination.

    Are your products tested for quality?

    Every batch undergoes compressive strength, dimensional accuracy and water absorption testing before dispatch, in line with relevant IS standards.

    Can I get custom sizes or specifications?

    Yes, we offer custom sizing on several product lines including boundary pillars and precast slabs — share your requirement via the quote form.

    How do I request a formal quotation?

    Fill out the quote form below with your product and quantity requirements — our team responds within one business day via WhatsApp or email.
